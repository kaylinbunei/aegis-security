Company overview

AEGIS Escort Firm is a professional security service provider specializing in escort operations, close protection, and secure movement solutions across Papua New Guinea.

We are built on a foundation of discipline, operational experience, and reliability, delivering tailored security solutions to corporate organizations, government agencies, NGOs, and private clients operating in both urban and high-risk environments.

Our mission: to ensure safe, controlled, and disruption-free movement for every client we serve.

Our team

Our strength lies in our personnel.

AEGIS is composed of an elite team of current and retired Papua New Guinea Defence Force (PNGDF) soldiers, bringing real-world operational experience, strategic awareness, and disciplined execution to every assignment.
